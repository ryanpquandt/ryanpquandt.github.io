Ryan Phillip Quandt

Applied Economist and Philosopher

My recent research examines sunset legislation and regulatory review, the mission commitment of nonprofit hospitals, and how cognitive friction in human-AI teams can improve team performance. My work has appeared in the Journal of Benefit-Cost Analysis, the Journal of Regulatory Economics, Argument & Computation, and Frontiers in Physics, along with volumes from Springer and Routledge. I’m currently serving as a Senior Economist at the White House’s Council of Economic Advisers.
